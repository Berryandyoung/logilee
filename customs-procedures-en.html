🛃 Customs Procedures

This guide covers the essential steps involved in the customs clearance process for international trade.

📑 1. Import Declaration

Submit necessary documents to the customs authority, including:

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB)
  • Import Licenses (if required)

🔍 2. Customs Inspection

Customs officers may inspect the goods to verify their nature, value, and compliance with regulations.

💰 3. Tax & Duty Payment

Pay applicable import duties, Value Added Tax (VAT), and other related fees before the goods are released.

✅ 4. Final Clearance

Once all inspections are passed and duties are paid, the goods are cleared for delivery to the final destination.
