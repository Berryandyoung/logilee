EU Plans 2026 Revision of Dual-Use Control List

The European Union plans to revise the dual-use item control list in Annex I to Regulation 2021/821.

2026-09-16 2 min read Share

The European Union plans to revise the dual-use item control list in Annex I to Regulation 2021/821. The revision is expected to reflect 2025 decisions by the Wassenaar Arrangement, Australia Group and Nuclear Suppliers Group, followed by a planned two-month scrutiny period by the European Parliament and the Council before publication in the Official Journal.

Key developments

What is changing

The European Union plans to revise the list of dual-use items in Annex I to Regulation 2021/821. The revision is expected to incorporate decisions adopted in 2025 by the Wassenaar Arrangement, Australia Group and Nuclear Suppliers Group.

The supplied announcement does not identify the individual items, control numbers or technical specifications that would change. It is therefore not possible, on the available information, to determine whether a particular product will become newly controlled.

Timing and procedure

Before publication in the Official Journal of the European Union, the draft revision is expected to undergo a two-month scrutiny period by the European Parliament and the Council. The supplied material does not establish the Official Journal publication date or the date on which the revised list would begin to apply.

Operational relevance

Exporters, importers, sellers and logistics or trade teams whose transactions may be covered by the EU dual-use rules should monitor the revision process. Because no item-level amendments or technical requirements have been provided, the available information does not support a new conclusion on whether a specific transaction requires authorization.

Checks for businesses

  • Review whether products and transaction flows may relate to the controlled-item list in Annex I to Regulation 2021/821.
  • Track the revision process and its incorporation of the 2025 decisions by the Wassenaar Arrangement, Australia Group and Nuclear Suppliers Group.
  • After the two-month scrutiny period, verify whether the revision has been published in the Official Journal and whether an application date has been established.
  • Do not process transactions on the assumption that a particular control number or authorization requirement has changed before the final revision is available.

The announcement confirms a planned list revision and scrutiny process. It does not, on the supplied facts, confirm individual item changes or an effective date.
