Commercial Invoice Common Mistakes

A Commercial Invoice is a reference point for customs, payment, and transport documents. Check it against related documents and terms before shipment.

2026.08.146 min readTrade Documents

Terms to verify first

A Commercial Invoice is a transaction document issued by the seller. In practice, it should be checked against the Packing List, ocean B/L, or air AWB.

Classification starts with the HS Code, while cost and risk scope should match the stated Incoterms.

Seven common mistakes

  1. Buyer or seller details do not match the contract.
  2. Goods, quantity, unit price, or currency differ from the PO.
  3. Packing details do not match the Packing List.
  4. Shipper, consignee, or notify party differ from B/L or AWB.
  5. Goods are listed without HS Code review.
  6. Incoterms do not match freight scope.
  7. Origin, payment terms, invoice number, or date are missing.
Treat the Commercial Invoice as a control document that connects transaction, packing, transport, and customs information.

Disclaimer

This is a practical checklist. Confirm final customs, tax, payment, and documentation decisions with your contract, customs broker, forwarder, and relevant authorities.
