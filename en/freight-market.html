How to read these indicators

The Freight Transportation Services Index measures the volume of services performed by the U.S. for-hire freight transportation sector. Related component fields help show truck, rail, air, and waterborne movement signals, but they are statistical series with update delays and revisions.

Use this page as market context for logistics planning, not as a freight quotation source. Source: U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics.
