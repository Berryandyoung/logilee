Tracking & Reference Sources

ISO 6346

Used for container number structure and check digit validation. It does not confirm actual container existence or shipment status.

BIC Register

Owner codes identify registered container owners, not necessarily the operating carrier.

IATA AWB

Used for the 3-digit airline prefix and 8-digit serial/check digit structure.

Live tracking data
