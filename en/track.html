Tracking Launcher

Shipment Tracking Launcher

Enter a courier, B/L, container, or AWB reference to open a supported official carrier tracking page. LOGILEE is a tracking launcher and does not generate live shipment status, vessel positions, or schedules.

Courier tracking uses parcel numbers. B/L tracking uses an ocean bill of lading reference. Container tracking uses the container number when the carrier supports it. Air cargo tracking usually requires an 11-digit AWB and may need the airline's own cargo portal.
