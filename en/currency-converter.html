Using exchange rates in trade work

Frankfurter provides public reference exchange rates. Actual buying, selling, card, bank, and remittance rates can differ because providers apply spreads and update at different times.

For contracts, clarify whether the benchmark is invoice date, payment date, customs declaration date, or another agreed rate date.
