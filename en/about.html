About LOGILEE

A global trade workspace that helps practitioners move from research to document, customs, and logistics decisions.

Who LOGILEE is for

LOGILEE is built for import and export teams, logistics operators, freight-forwarding practitioners, cross-border sellers, and people learning international trade. Posts, the dictionary, practical tools, document templates, and learning guides are designed as connected parts of the same workflow.

Use Posts for recent changes and practical explanations, the Dictionary and guides for background, and the tools and templates to continue the task.

How we approach content

For regulatory, tariff, and customs updates, LOGILEE prefers primary or authoritative material from governments, customs authorities, and international organizations when it is available. Articles aim to distinguish who is affected, relevant dates, checks to perform, and practical next steps, while preserving links to the original sources.

Published material may be reviewed when official guidance changes, links fail, or dates and explanations need correction. LOGILEE provides general information, not legal, tax, or customs advice. Before filing, contracting, or making a compliance decision, confirm the current requirements with the responsible authority or a qualified customs, freight, or professional adviser.

Corrections and contact

If you find outdated or inaccurate information or a broken link, use the contact page and include the page URL and the issue you found. A link to an official source is helpful when available.
