Trade Documents Hub

Understand what each trade document does, who issues it, when it is used, and where mistakes often happen.

Invoice

Commercial Invoice

What it is: sales and customs value document. Who issues it: seller/exporter. Key fields: parties, goods, value, currency, Incoterms, origin. Common mistakes: mismatched values, unclear terms, missing origin.

Packing

Packing List

Shows package count, weights, dimensions, and marks. Used by forwarders, customs brokers, warehouses, and consignees to match physical cargo.

Ocean

Bill of Lading (B/L)

Carrier-issued evidence of receipt or shipment. Check shipper, consignee, notify party, vessel, voyage, container, and freight terms carefully.

Air

Air Waybill (AWB)

Air cargo transport document and tracking reference. Common mistakes include wrong consignee details, chargeable weight, or airport codes.

Origin

Certificate of Origin

Supports origin declarations, FTA checks, or buyer/import requirements. Requirements vary by country and program.

Quotation

Proforma Invoice

Pre-shipment quotation-style document often used before payment, license checks, or buyer approval. It is not always a tax invoice.
