What counts as a business day

For this tool, a business day means a weekday that is not listed as a public holiday for the selected country. It is a practical planning rule for shipment lead time, banking, customs clearance, document review, and supplier response expectations.

Real logistics schedules may still differ. Port terminals, courier depots, customs brokers, and overseas offices can have separate cutoffs, regional closures, or emergency operating notices. Use this result as a planning reference and confirm time-sensitive milestones with the responsible party.
