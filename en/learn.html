FAQ

Common Questions

What is the difference between FOB and CIF?

FOB and CIF divide cost and risk differently. Start with the Incoterms guide and compare related terms in the dictionary.

Why is an original B/L important?

A negotiable original B/L can control cargo release. Confirm the release method with the carrier, forwarder, buyer, and bank before shipment.

Who determines the HS Code?

The importer of record is typically responsible for customs declaration accuracy, but exporters often help with product details. Use official tariff references for confirmation.

What is the difference between demurrage and detention?

Demurrage usually relates to containers staying too long at the terminal, while detention usually relates to containers held outside the terminal after pickup.

How are Commercial Invoice and Proforma Invoice different?

A proforma invoice is commonly used before final shipment or payment, while a commercial invoice supports actual customs and accounting workflows.

Should I choose FCL or LCL?

Compare cargo volume, timing, handling risk, and cost. The CBM Calculator helps estimate shipment volume, but it does not confirm loading feasibility.
