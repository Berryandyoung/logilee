Reading the profile

The profile combines several indicators that are commonly used before deeper country research: population, GDP, GDP growth, inflation, trade as a share of GDP, exports, and imports. Because each indicator can be updated at a different time, LOGILEE displays the latest available year separately for every row.

Use this as a starting point for market screening, supplier country comparisons, and freight demand context. It should not replace local regulations, current tariff checks, buyer credit review, or real-time market intelligence.
