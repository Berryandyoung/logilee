Country Trade Profile

Select a country to review key economy and trade indicators from public World Bank data.

What this profile covers

Select a country to review a country overview, location map, World Bank economic and trade indicators, export-import balance, related LOGILEE tools, and registered major ports in one workflow.

This page is not a country encyclopedia. It is a starting point for trade teams moving from country screening to logistics routing, customs checks, and document preparation.

Before a transaction, confirm current regulations, tariffs, customs requirements, and counterparty credit through official or professional channels.

Select a country to load World Bank indicators and country metadata.

How to use this profile

Population and GDP help separate market scale from trade activity. Trade share, exports, and imports help show how much external trade appears in the available data. Each World Bank indicator can have a different latest available year, so review the year shown in both cards and the detailed table.

Continue the workflow with LOGILEE public holidays, ports, currency, HS Code, and EU Trade tools where relevant.

Data source notes

Economic and trade indicators and country basics use World Bank data. Major language and currency-name references use LOGILEE local reference data, maps use OpenStreetMap, and flag images use FlagCDN. If an API is temporarily unavailable, LOGILEE shows a fallback state without blocking the entire page.
