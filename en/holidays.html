Planning around public holidays

Holiday calendars help teams avoid unrealistic pickup, customs, and payment deadlines. A factory holiday, bank closure, customs office closure, or local port administration break can change the practical lead time even when vessels and trucks continue operating.

Use the calendar as an early planning check, then confirm with the forwarder, carrier, customs broker, and consignee for local cutoffs. Some countries also have regional holidays or substituted workdays that can affect individual offices.
