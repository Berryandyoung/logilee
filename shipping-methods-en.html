🚢 Shipping Methods

This guide introduces various shipping options available for international trade.

🛳️ 1. Sea Freight

Sea freight is the most cost-effective option for transporting large quantities of goods over long distances. Common shipping terms include:

  • FCL (Full Container Load)
  • LCL (Less than Container Load)

✈️ 2. Air Freight

Air freight is ideal for high-value, time-sensitive, or perishable goods. While more expensive than sea freight, it offers faster delivery times.

📦 3. Express Delivery

Express delivery services like DHL, FedEx, and UPS offer fast, door-to-door solutions for small packages and urgent shipments.
