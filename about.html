About LogiLee

LogiLee is a practical logistics platform built for importers, exporters, and logistics professionals. We provide easy-to-use tools (like CBM calculation and tracking shortcuts) and simple guides that help you understand real-world shipping processes.

Our goal is to make logistics tasks faster, clearer, and more reliable—so you can focus on execution, not searching for scattered information.

What You Can Do Here

  • CBM Calculator: Quickly calculate volume and estimate cargo space.
  • Tracking: Jump to major courier / carrier tracking pages.
  • Logistics Guides: Clear explanations for Incoterms, documents, and shipping basics.

Who This Site Is For

  • Trade & logistics coordinators
  • Freight forwarding and operations teams
  • Small business owners importing/exporting goods
  • Anyone learning international shipping

How We Keep It Useful

We aim to publish content that is actionable and easy to verify. Information may vary by country, carrier, and local regulations—so please use LogiLee as a guide and double-check details with your forwarder, customs broker, or official sources when needed.
